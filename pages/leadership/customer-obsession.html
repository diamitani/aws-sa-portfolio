LP 01 AWS Leadership Principle

Customer Obsession

"Leaders start with the customer and work backwards. They work vigorously to earn and keep customer trust."

S Situation

Our sales reps needed a customized pre-call brief to review before every client meeting. They already had access to raw datapoints—web activity, HubSpot engagement, past meeting notes—but that data lived in silos and required manual digging.

💡 What they actually needed was synthesized insight, not another dashboard of numbers.
T Task

I was asked to build a workflow automation that combined company and prospect research, lead intelligence, and HubSpot activity signals into a single, actionable brief for reps ahead of every call.

Marketing leadership and the board separately requested the same kind of insight generated automatically for every Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL), so I owned both builds.

A Action
01

Data Source Mapping

Mapped every data source I'd need: HubSpot contact and company activity, prior meeting history (where available), Factors.ai intent data from our existing subscription, and our internal product/service catalog for matching relevance.

02

n8n Workflow Build

Built an n8n workflow that pulled scheduled meetings from HubSpot daily and enriched each one with contact- and company-level data.

03

Prompt Engineering

The hardest part was prompt engineering the AI node so it moved beyond surface-level summaries—for example, correctly reading a spike in page views and mapping it to the right Atlas product (like flagging interest in Argentina regulatory content and tying it to the relevant compliance offering).

04

Benchmark Validation

To validate accuracy, I manually pulled a sample of high-activity contacts (identified with Claude), reviewed their page views and actions myself, and wrote my own analysis as a benchmark to calibrate what "good" output looked like for the AI.

05

Stakeholder Review & Ship

Reviewed the approach with my manager and the marketing team, refined the prompt logic until it consistently matched that benchmark, then built the rep-facing and MQL-facing versions in parallel and shipped both to production.

R Result
5-bullet Action Brief Per Contact
100% MQL Coverage
Daily Auto-Generated

The finished workflow pulls signal from contact messages, web activity, and company/contact research to auto-generate a concise 5-bullet action brief for every prospect and MQL, mapped directly to our relevant products and services.

It's now embedded as a standard view on every contact record in HubSpot, giving reps and marketing a consistent, always-current source of insight instead of a one-off summary—directly reducing the manual research time reps previously spent per call.
