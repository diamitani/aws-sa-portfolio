LP 05 AWS Leadership Principle

Learn and Be Curious

"Leaders are never done learning and always seek to improve themselves. They are curious about new possibilities and act to explore them."

S Situation

When I first entered the AI space, I was in the same position as most newcomers: constantly consuming daily updates, trending tools, and new research just to keep pace with a field that was changing week to week.

💡 I wanted a way to turn that constant consumption into something more structured—a habit of writing and publishing that would also position me as a thought leader rather than just a passive follower of the space.
T Task

I set out to build and publish a LinkedIn newsletter, LiveBuildAI, committing to a 30-day sprint where I would research, write, and post a new AI-focused article every single day.

A Action
01

Platform Selection

Deliberately chose the simplest distribution channel available to me: LinkedIn, where I already had an engaged following, rather than building a new platform or audience from scratch.

02

Content System Design

To make daily publishing sustainable, I used Google's AI news feed as my primary source of raw material and built a custom GPT trained to help me draft each article in a consistent format—top five stories of the day, a featured tool from a running curated list, and a video of the day.

03

Daily Execution

Published one entry every day from March 1 through March 30.

04

Distribution Amplification

Drove distribution beyond the newsletter itself by cross-posting to X and relevant LinkedIn groups, and resharing to my main feed to compound reach.

🔄 The daily cadence forced me to stay current on the field in real time rather than learning in occasional bursts, which sharpened both my grasp of the AI landscape and my ability to communicate it clearly and quickly.
R Result
992+ Subscribers in 30 Days
30 Daily Articles Published
Inbound Business Leads Generated

The newsletter grew to over 992 subscribers in 30 days and became a recognizable, recurring source of daily AI news for my network.

Beyond subscriber growth, it generated inbound interest from readers requesting custom AI project proposals—turning a personal learning habit into a channel that produced real business leads.
