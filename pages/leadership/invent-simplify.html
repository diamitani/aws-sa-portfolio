LP 03 AWS Leadership Principle

Invent and Simplify

"Leaders expect and require innovation and invention from their teams and always find ways to simplify. They are externally aware, look for new ideas from everywhere, and are not limited by 'not invented here.'"

S Situation

Our VP of Growth wanted to eliminate roughly two hours a day that account executives were spending on manual prospecting and research.

🎯 His vision was an end-to-end automation that could research leads against defined criteria, identify the right contacts, build a pain-point analysis for each one, write personalized outreach emails, and send them on the reps' behalf—so AEs could spend their time calling warm leads instead of researching cold ones.
T Task

I took on building a fully automated pipeline that would run daily, research and qualify new leads, generate AI-personalized messaging for each one, and enroll them into an outreach sequence without any manual rep involvement.

A Action
01

Daily Trigger + Scoring

Designed a workflow that triggered every morning at 7am and scanned HubSpot for companies that had crossed into "Hot" or "Warm" status based on our Factors.AI and ZoomInfo scoring criteria.

02

Smart Filtering

The workflow filtered out any accounts that were already active opportunities or open deals, then mapped the remaining leads to rep territories using round-robin assignment logic.

03

Credit-Conscious Contact Discovery

Combined an HTTP GET request with an n8n-to-Clay webhook that queried Amplemarket and Clay for contact data, then ranked contacts by strength so we only spent enrichment credits revealing the people most likely to convert—rather than paying to unmask every contact at a company.

04

AI Research + Pain-Point Mapping

For each qualified contact, I wrote system instructions for an AI node that researched the company and prospect online, formed a pain-point hypothesis, and mapped it to how our product could address that specific need.

05

Personalized Sequence Generation

That analysis then drove AI-generated copy for a three-email sequence, tailored to each contact rather than templated.

06

Multi-Tool Routing

Routed the finished sequences two ways: into a HubSpot list for reps working leads inside HubSpot, and directly into Amplemarket's outreach tool via a Clay API column push for reps working from that platform.

R Result
7am Daily Auto-Run
3-Email Personalized Sequence
$0 Wasted Enrichment

The result is a fully automated messaging engine that sources new leads daily from external platforms, enriches and ranks contacts through Clay and Amplemarket, generates personalized research and copy with AI, and pushes finished sequences straight into the tools reps already use.

Removed the manual research and writing work the VP had originally targeted and giving AEs a daily queue of warm, pre-qualified leads ready to call.

The "Simplify" Half:

The design stayed resource-conscious by only spending enrichment credits on the highest-ranked contacts rather than unmasking every lead indiscriminately—reflecting the "simplify" half of the principle alongside the invention itself.
