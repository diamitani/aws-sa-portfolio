STAR Case Study

S · Situation The problem
Turn/River's PE portfolio companies (SolarWinds, Invicti, Commio, and 4 others) needed compliant, auditable outbound sales at scale — but each operated as a separate entity with its own ICP, suppression lists, and approval chains. Prior vendor tools had sent unauthorized emails at portfolio companies, creating regulatory exposure. Legal teams were blocking AI adoption. The operating teams needed an AI SDR they could actually trust.
T · Task The objective
Design and build a multi-tenant AI SDR platform that: (1) served all 7 portfolio companies from a single system with zero cross-tenant data leakage, (2) enforced human approval before every external action, (3) was transparent enough for legal sign-off in one meeting, and (4) could be demo-ready for PE review within weeks.
A · Action What I built
Designed governance-first: the Compliance Gate agent became the architectural center — suppression enforcement before staging, approval events required before any external action, dry-run preview on scope changes.

Built 8 specialized agents with clear role separation: research agents produce artifacts but cannot perform side effects — only the NPAO Orchestrator manages run state. This is the single most important design decision: it made the system provably safe to legal teams because side effects were physically isolated.

Integrated 250+ tools via Composio MCP (Gmail, LinkedIn, HubSpot, Slack, Clay, n8n) through one connector hub. Frontend on Next.js with Vercel SDK for clean handoff. Backend on AWS Bedrock Agent Core with Supabase for workspace state, ICPs, leads, runs, approvals, and audit logs.
R · Result Measured outcomes
7 active portfolio workspaces operational — each with its own brief, ICP, data-provider binding, approvers, and suppression rules. Zero compliance incidents since launch. Legal teams approved the platform on first review — the governance model became a selling point, not just a compliance checkbox.

Demo success path: "Build an ICP for our workflow software, research 10 VP Operations prospects, create a 4-step sequence" → ICP card + prioritized lead table + evidence labels → approve → Stage 10 contacts → audit event. No email sent without approval.

🔐 Binding Governance Model

These are not guidelines — they are architectural constraints enforced in code.

✓ No external sending / enrollment / CRM-write / workflow activation without an approval event logged in the audit trail.
✓ ENV:-only secrets — never committed, never embedded in agent prompts, never pasted in chat.
✓ Source + confidence labeling on all AI-generated content — never present inferred data as fact.
✓ Suppression / do-not-contact enforced before any staging action, at the Compliance Gate level.
✓ Dry-run preview + re-approval on any scope / contact-count / destination / sequence change.
✓ Closed context per workspace — portfolio companies see only their own data. Zero cross-company knowledge leakage.
