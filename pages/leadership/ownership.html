LP 02 AWS Leadership Principle

Ownership

"Leaders are owners. They think long-term and don't sacrifice long-term value for short-term results. They act on behalf of the entire company, beyond just their own team. They never say 'that's not my job.'"

S Situation

RevOps needed to clean up the HubSpot database, which had become inconsistent due to reps manually uploading leads without following a standard process—skipping key properties, mislabeling fields, and creating downstream data hygiene issues.

RevOps responded by building a standardized import template for all reps to use going forward. But the rollout introduced a new training burden, and several reps struggled to adopt the template correctly inside HubSpot.

T Task

Rather than let the new template become another underused process, I took ownership of closing that adoption gap.

🎯 I set out to build a Claude Cowork skill that would let reps upload or search for leads and have them automatically formatted and imported into HubSpot using the RevOps template, removing the manual effort and guesswork that was causing errors in the first place.
A Action
01

Cross-Team Alignment

Looped in RevOps early to align on the project and get their input and testing support, since they owned the template and the data standards it enforced.

02

Skill Architecture

Using a custom skill-builder project in Claude Cowork—configured to design skills against best-practice documentation as its knowledge base—I directed it to build a skill that would: transform a rep's raw lead list into the correctly formatted template, connect to our HubSpot integration to import the records on the rep's behalf, and automatically create a new list to store each import.

03

Validation Loop

To validate the skill, I uploaded the RevOps template and tested it against multiple real lead lists downloaded from Amplemarket, our lead data provider. I iterated on the skill inside the Claude Cowork platform, running repeated test imports until the output consistently met RevOps's formatting standards.

04

Ship & Rollout

Once it performed reliably, I shipped it and rolled it out to the broader sales organization.

R Result
0 Template Errors
100% Data Compliance
Auto List Creation

The skill now saves reps meaningful time on every lead import during prospecting, while keeping HubSpot data clean and consistent without requiring reps to master the template manually.

Real Example:

A rep building an AI outreach sequence for a new ICP category used the skill to import his freshly sourced leads directly into a sequence-ready list—cutting what would have been a manual formatting step out of his workflow entirely and letting him move straight from prospecting to outreach.
